# Data modalities and exploratory data analysis

## AI as research
AI research follows the scientific method. The steps are observation, hypothesis, experimentation, analysis and conclusion. The research pipeline runs from problem formulation to data collection, model development, evaluation and validation, and finally knowledge contribution.

## Data modalities
Tabular data has rows as entities and columns as features. Time series data is ordered. Text is profiled through tokens, vocabulary and length. Images are profiled by shape, aspect ratio and channels. Audio is profiled by sampling rate, duration and spectrograms. Video adds frame rate, resolution and colour depth.

## What EDA is
Exploratory data analysis (EDA) profiles, summarises and visualises data before modelling. It uses simple statistics and plots. It finds missing values, duplicates, outliers, imbalance, leakage and bias. It also guides model choice and baselines.

## Tabular data checks
Check schema and integrity for duplicate rows and constant columns. Check missing values to plan imputation. Check distributions and outliers, and tell errors from true extremes. Check feature scales, because distance-based models are sensitive to them. Correlation between features shows redundancy, and a correlation with the target that is too perfect suggests leakage. Multicollinearity shows how well each feature is explained by the other features. Target relationships show which features are informative. Class imbalance calls for stratified splits and metrics such as F1. PCA on standardised data shows how far the data can be compressed and which axes are noisy.

## Choosing train, validation and test splits
Random or stratified splits suit independent rows. Time-based splits suit data over time. Grouped splits keep records of the same entity together. A wrong split gives optimistic results.

## Time series checks
Look at continuity and gaps, seasonality, stationarity, autocorrelation, and anomalies or change points. Stationarity means the mean and variance stay stable over time. Differencing, detrending and log transforms can help when they do not.

## Text checks
Stop-word removal cuts noise. Case normalisation reduces sparsity, but case can carry meaning. Cleansing should keep the meaning of the text. Also look at corpus size, length distributions, vocabulary, n-grams, noise and embeddings.

## Image checks
Profile resolution and aspect ratio. Check channel statistics, blur and corrupt files. Find duplicates across splits with hashes or embeddings, because they leak information.
