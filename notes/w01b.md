# Data modalities and exploratory data analysis

## AI as research
AI research follows the scientific method. The steps are observation, hypothesis, experimentation, analysis and conclusion. The research pipeline runs from problem formulation to data collection, model development, evaluation and validation, and finally knowledge contribution.

## Data modalities
Tabular data has rows as entities and columns as features. Time series data is ordered. Text is profiled through tokens, vocabulary and length. Images are profiled by shape, ratio and channels. Audio is profiled by sampling rate, duration and spectrograms. Video adds frame rate, resolution and colour depth.

## What EDA is
Exploratory data analysis profiles, summarises and visualises data before modelling. It uses simple statistics and plots. It finds missing values, duplicates, outliers, imbalance, leakage and bias. It also guides model choice and baselines.

## Tabular data checks
Check schema and integrity for duplicates and constant columns. Check missingness to plan imputation. Check distributions and outliers, and tell errors from true extremes. Check scaling because distance-based models are sensitive to it. Correlation shows redundancy and leakage suspects. Multicollinearity shows how well each feature is explained by the others. Target relationships show which features are informative. Class imbalance calls for stratified splits and metrics such as F1.

## Split design
Random or stratified splits suit independent rows. Time-based splits suit data over time. Grouped splits keep records of the same entity together. A wrong split gives optimistic results. PCA gives a quick look at compression and noisy axes.

## Time series checks
Look at continuity and gaps, seasonality, stationarity, autocorrelation, and anomalies or change points. Stationarity means mean and variance stay stable. Differencing and log transforms can help.

## Text checks
Stop-word removal cuts noise. Case normalisation reduces sparsity but case can carry meaning. Cleansing should keep meaning. Also look at corpus size, length distributions, vocabulary, n-grams, noise and embeddings.

## Image checks
Profile resolution and aspect ratio. Check channel statistics, blur and corrupt files. Find duplicates across splits with hashes or embeddings, since they leak information.
