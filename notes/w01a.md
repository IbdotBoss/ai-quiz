# What AI is and the main types of AI

## Defining AI
John McCarthy coined the term in 1956. AI is the science and engineering of making intelligent machines. Artificial means produced by human effort, and most people agree on that meaning. Intelligence is very hard to define because it is hard to measure. AI does not have to copy biologically observable methods.

## The Turing Test
Turing replaced the question "Can machines think?" with one that can be tested, "Can a machine pass a behaviour test for intelligence?" A human judge talks to a hidden entity. If the judge cannot tell whether it is a computer, the computer passes and is said to be intelligent. The test looks at behaviour and ignores how the machine works.

## Multiple intelligences
The theory says intelligence is split into several kinds and is not one general ability. The kinds are visual-spatial, emotional or interpersonal, intrapersonal, linguistic-verbal, logical-mathematical, bodily-kinesthetic, naturalistic and existential. Each kind raises the question of whether a machine could have it.

## Weak AI and strong AI
Weak AI solves a specific problem with predefined instructions and parameters. Almost all of today's AI is weak, such as Siri, Alexa, chatbots and self driving cars. Strong AI would equal human intelligence, with self awareness, consciousness, creativity and planning. It exists only as a theoretical idea.

## Four types of AI
| Type | Key idea |
|---|---|
| Reactive machines | No memory, so the same input gives the same output, as in Deep Blue |
| Limited memory | Looks at the past and improves with more training data, as in deep learning |
| Theory of mind | Understands human motives and intentions and predicts behaviour |
| Self-awareness | Has a sense of self and does not exist yet |

## Weak and strong AI compared with the four types
Weak and strong AI split AI by how broad its ability is. The four types split it by memory and understanding. They are two separate lists, so a type such as reactive machines is not a third option beside weak and strong.

## The computing definition of AI
In a computing context, AI is the ability of a machine to mimic the behaviour of living things, mainly humans. How the machine does this does not matter. The outcome is what counts.
